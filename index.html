Privacy Policy

Your notes are not sent to Arch Builder. Optional rewarded ads use Google Mobile Ads and Google privacy choices. Optional paid Premium is processed by Google Play.

Effective date
9 August 2026
Developer
Arch Builder
01

Notes stay local

Canvases, handwriting, folders, and files are not sent to Arch Builder.

02

Ads are optional

No banners or forced ads. Rewarded ads start only after you choose them.

03

Privacy choices

Google UMP presents consent and privacy options where required.

01

Information stored on your device

ArchRill stores note content, canvas or notebook type, page count and dimensions, strokes, links, imported documents and images, folder names, camera position, drawing settings, input and export preferences, appearance choices, App Lock, and locally cached paid Premium information in private device storage.

Rewarded-Premium information consists of whether you chose to prepare rewarded ads, the number of completed six-hour segments in the current cycle, and the local expiry time. Arch Builder cannot access or recover your locally stored notes or settings.

02

Data collection and sharing

Arch Builder does not operate an ArchRill account, note-content server, cloud-sync service, or developer analytics service. ArchRill does not send canvases, handwriting, imported or exported files, folder names, biometric credentials, contacts, or purchase tokens to Arch Builder.

If you voluntarily choose rewarded ads, ArchRill uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google states that this SDK automatically collects and shares IP address information, product interactions such as app launches, taps and ad video views, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. IP address information may estimate approximate location. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit. Google's terms and privacy policy apply.

03

Rewarded ads and temporary tool access

Rewarded ads are optional and begin only after you choose the rewarded-ad option. ArchRill does not show banner ads, automatic ads, or forced ads. Each reward confirmed after a completed ad adds six hours of temporary access to Premium pens, Favorite Zoom, Custom Paint, and the toolbar colour. Up to four rewards can be stacked in one cycle for 24 hours, together or one at a time.

Rewarded access does not increase the Free limits for Infinite canvases, Finite notebooks, or folder depth, and does not unlock gallery-imported page templates, the app-wide theme colour, or App Lock. Those persistent features require a paid Premium plan or debug-only tester access.

Google controls ad availability, content, format, and the exact reward countdown. ArchRill grants time only when Google reports that the reward was earned. Closing early adds no time. When access expires, the temporary writing and toolbar tools return to Free unless another valid Premium method is active. Existing notes remain available.

04

Ad consent and privacy choices

ArchRill uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request the current consent status and show a consent or privacy message where required. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is not initialized for a user who has never chosen rewarded ads. After that choice, ArchRill requests updated consent information on later launches and requests an ad only when Google's consent system permits it.

Where required, Settings shows Ad privacy choices to reopen Google's form. Consent can affect personalized, non-personalized, limited, or technical ad serving. Ads can be unavailable because of consent, network, region, age, account, inventory, or policy requirements.

05

Premium purchases and Google Play Billing

ArchRill uses Google Play Billing for optional Lifetime, Yearly, and Monthly plans. Google Play displays local prices, processes payment, and manages renewal and cancellation under Google's terms and privacy policy. ArchRill never receives or stores card numbers, bank information, or Google passwords.

Google Play provides product identifier, purchase state, acknowledgement state, and a purchase token. ArchRill uses these only to confirm, restore, and acknowledge paid Premium. The token is not written to notes or sent to Arch Builder. Only the paid plan and last verification time are stored locally.

06

App lock and Android authentication

If App Lock is enabled, Android verifies a fingerprint or device screen-lock credential. ArchRill receives only success or failure and does not receive, store, collect, or transmit biometric data, PINs, patterns, or passwords.

App Lock controls access to the interface only and does not encrypt canvas files. The preference stays on the device and can be turned off after Android verifies the user.

07

Imports, exports, and sharing

ArchRill accesses a document or image only after selection through Android's picker. An export is written only to the chosen location. If an exported file is shared through another app or service, that third party's privacy practices apply. ArchRill does not automatically upload or share files.

08

Android backup and device transfer

Depending on Android settings, the operating system or chosen backup provider may include ArchRill data in a backup or device transfer. Those features are controlled by Android and the backup provider, not Arch Builder.

09

Retention and deletion

Local content remains until it is deleted, app data is cleared, or ArchRill is uninstalled. Rewarded-access information stays locally until expiry or app-data clearing. Exported files stay at their selected location until deleted. Arch Builder does not hold a server-side note copy and cannot delete or restore notes remotely.

Data processed by Google for ads or billing is retained under Google's applicable terms, controls, and privacy policy.

10

Children's privacy

ArchRill is not directed to children under 13. Arch Builder does not knowingly collect note content or personal information directly from children. Google may process advertising-related data when rewarded ads are chosen. Play target-audience and AdMob settings must match the ages selected for distribution.

11

Policy updates

If ArchRill's data practices change, the public and in-app policies will be updated before publication. The effective date identifies the current version.

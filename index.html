Privacy Policy

ArchRill keeps your canvases and preferences on your device. Arch Builder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

01

No collection

Your notes and drawings are not sent to Arch Builder.

02

No tracking

There are no analytics, advertising, accounts, or trackers.

03

You stay in control

You choose what to import, export, share, or delete.

01

Information stored on your device

ArchRill stores canvas content, strokes, links, imported documents and images, folder names, camera position, drawing settings, input preferences, export preferences, and appearance choices in the app's private storage on your device.

This information is used only to provide the app's note, drawing, workspace, import, and export features. Arch Builder cannot access or recover this locally stored content.

02

Data collection and sharing

ArchRill does not collect or share personal information, device identifiers, usage information, diagnostics, location, contacts, financial information, or any other user data. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, account services, or tracking SDKs.

03

Imports, exports, and sharing

ArchRill accesses a document or image only after you select it through Android's system file picker. An exported file is written only to the location you choose.

If you share an exported file using another app or service, that third party's privacy practices apply. ArchRill does not automatically upload or share your files.

04

Android backup and device transfer

Depending on your Android settings, the operating system or your selected backup provider may include ArchRill app data in a device backup or device-to-device transfer. Those features are controlled by Android and the backup provider, not by Arch Builder.

05

Retention and deletion

Local content remains on your device until you delete a canvas or folder, clear ArchRill's app data, or uninstall the app. Files you exported remain in the location you selected until you delete them there. Backup copies are governed by your Android or backup-provider settings.

Because ArchRill has no account or server, Arch Builder does not hold a server-side copy and cannot delete or restore your content remotely.

06

Children's privacy

ArchRill does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. All app content remains subject to the same on-device privacy protections described in this policy.

07

Policy updates

If ArchRill's data practices change, this policy and the in-app copy will be updated before the changed version is published. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
